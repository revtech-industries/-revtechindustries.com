FAQ

Straight answers

What is Artemis?

An intelligent wellness companion from REVTECH INDUSTRIES — app-based today, with optional hardware sensing as the Gaia Neural Band matures. It combines voice, journaling, quests, and biodata-aware coaching.

What is the Gaia Neural Band?

A screenless wearable prototype that streams biometric and wellness signals into Artemis. It evolved from early black-cylinder sensor rigs into a soft band form factor with a cyan status ring.

Is my data private?

Sensitive mental health data is designed for local-first handling. Partnerships and cloud features stay explicit — we do not treat your journal as marketing fuel.

Which platforms?

Android and Windows betas. iOS is deferred while we harden the Android companion experience.
